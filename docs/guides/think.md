# Have Lumina think for you

Four surfaces that produce something rather than retrieve it.

| | What it gives you |
|---|---|
| [Reflect](#reflect) | A synthesis across everything you have saved |
| [Notebook](#notebook) | A written-up answer on a topic you name |
| [Insights](#insights) | Things Lumina noticed without being asked |
| [Today](#today) | What happened, in one screen |

---

## Reflect

Three skills that read across your whole vault.

- **Patterns** — *"Find themes across your notes"*
- **Contradictions** — *"Spot clashing ideas"*
- **Beliefs** — *"Trace stance evolution"*

**Web** — Sidebar → **Reflect**. Pick a card, optionally give it a topic, click
**Reflect**.

**iOS** — **Insights** tab → the **Reflect** segment. Tap a card, fill the topic
sheet, tap **Run reflection**.

**Beliefs requires a topic** — it traces how your view on a specific thing
changed, so it has nothing to do without one. The other two take an optional
focus area. Both clients say so before you try.

It takes a moment: *"Reading across your vault. This one takes a moment."*

**Where it goes.** On the web the result is saved automatically and a footer
says **"Saved to \<path\>"**. On iOS you get **Save to Vault**, **Share** and
**Done** — saving is a choice, and it reuses the text already on screen rather
than re-running anything.

Past reflections list under the cards on iOS (**"Recent (last 10)"**) and appear
with every other skill output on **Skills** on the web.

Notes opened from the reflection list on iOS are **read-only** — no edit menu.

## Notebook

Ask Lumina to think something through and keep the write-up.

**Web** — Sidebar → **Notebook**. Type into
**"What should Lumina think about?"**, choose **Quick** or **Deep**, click
**Write memo**.

Deep runs a longer agent loop and takes longer. While it works:
*"Lumina is reading your vault and writing this up. A deep memo takes longer."*

The result renders under **Just written** and is saved to your vault — a memo
*is* a vault file, so clicking any row in the list opens it in the reader.

The sidebar label is **Notebook**; the URL is `/memos`. Same thing.

There is no iOS equivalent.

## Insights

Patterns, contradictions and connections Lumina found on its own, without you
asking.

**Web** — Sidebar → **Insights**. Cards in a grid. The only action is the **×**
to dismiss one — no confirmation, and it does not come back.

**iOS** — **Insights** tab → **Overview**, then the **PATTERNS** section. Tap a
row to read it. Before anything has accumulated:
*"Lumina will surface correlations and contradictions across these signals here."*

The web also shows a **Latest synthesis** card when there is one. If that call
fails the card just does not render.

## Today

**Web** — Sidebar → **Today**. Tabs: **Review**, **Achievements**, **Health**.

The Review tab shows the date, memories captured today, achievements, an output
streak, your **Skill outputs**, **Suggested actions** and **Recent achievements**.

Suggested actions are read-only cards. Nothing on this page is clickable, which
surprises people — it is a summary, not a to-do list.

**Health** tab: pick a **Metric** (Steps, Sleep, Heart rate) and click **Apply**
for a daily aggregate and recent events.

**iOS** — the Dashboard covers this ground instead. **More** → **Dashboard**,
then the **Command Deck** rows and the panels below.

## Insights vs Analytics

Easy to confuse:

- **Insights** — what Lumina noticed *in your content*.
- **Analytics** — how much you used it, what it cost, how healthy your memory
  is. Web: sidebar → **Analytics**. iOS: **Insights** tab → **Overview**.

Analytics is also where **Memory health** lives — a score out of 100 from
freshness (35%), engagement (25%), organization (20%) and review readiness
(20%). Its **Recommended next** cards are the only way into the memory review
queues; see [organise](organise.md#review-queues).

You can rate a model **Yes** / **No** under **Model effectiveness** on the web.
Once rated, the buttons are replaced by the word **Rated** — one rating per
model, no take-backs.
